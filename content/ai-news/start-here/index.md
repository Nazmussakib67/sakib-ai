**AI News & My Take** is a place for commentary on noteworthy reporting about artificial intelligence.

Each future news post will identify the original publisher and article, link readers to the source, distinguish reported facts from my own interpretation, and explain why the development matters. I will not reproduce entire articles or imply that another publication's reporting is my own.

This introductory post explains the editorial approach; it is not a report about a specific news event.
