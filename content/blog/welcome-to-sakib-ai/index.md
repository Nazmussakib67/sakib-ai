Artificial intelligence is moving quickly, and the most interesting questions are not always about the latest model or product. They are also about how these systems work, where they are useful, what their limitations are, and how they affect people.

I created **Sakib AI** as a space to explore those questions. Here, I plan to share my perspectives on AI research and technology, discuss noteworthy news with links to original reporting, and document ideas that emerge from my academic work.

This is an introduction to the publication, not a claim of completed research or an endorsement of any particular AI product. I hope to make complex topics more approachable while remaining careful about evidence, uncertainty, and real-world impact.

Thanks for reading.
